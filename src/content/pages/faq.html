  • A paid ad is content you pay a platform to show to strangers.

    Think of it this way: when someone scrolls through Facebook or Instagram, they see posts from friends and family. A paid ad shows up in that same feed, but it comes from a business. The business pays the platform to put it in front of specific people who are likely to care about what they offer.

    That content could be a video, a photo, or text. It shows someone a problem they have, a solution that exists, and a clear next step to take if they want it.

    The whole point of a paid ad is to get the right person to raise their hand.

  • It depends on where you're starting from.

    If you've run ads before, we have data to work with. We can move faster because we're not starting from zero. If this is your first campaign, the first few weeks are a learning period. The platform is figuring out who responds to your ads. That's normal, and it's necessary.

    Here's what you can count on: within the first week, you'll have real data. You'll see what's working, what isn't, and where people are dropping off. That's not nothing. That's the roadmap.

    Some businesses get leads in the first few days. Others take a few weeks to hit their stride. The difference usually comes down to ad spend, how competitive the market is, and how dialed in the offer is.

    What we don't do is guess. Every pivot we make is based on what the data is telling us. That's how you avoid wasting money chasing the wrong thing.

  • We make sure every lead lands somewhere you can act on it fast.

    Depending on your setup, leads flow directly into your CRM, your email inbox, or both. If you're running lead form ads, you'll get an instant notification the moment someone fills out a form. We also track everything in a shared lead tracker so nothing slips through the cracks.

    But here's the part that actually determines whether leads turn into revenue: speed to lead.

    Social media leads are not like referrals. They didn't ask a friend about you. They saw your ad, something clicked, and they raised their hand. That buying mindset has a short shelf life. The longer you wait to follow up, the colder they get.

    Call. Text. Email. Follow up more than feels comfortable. The businesses that win with paid ads are almost always the ones who treat every lead like it came from their best referral source.

    We'll set up the delivery side. The follow-up is on you, and it matters as much as the ads themselves.

  • For most local service businesses we work with, we recommend starting at $50 per day. That's roughly $1,500 a month going directly to the platform.

    That number isn't arbitrary. It's enough to gather real data quickly without burning through your budget while we're still in the learning phase. Go too low and the platform doesn't have enough to work with. Go too high before you have winning ads and you're just spending faster on the wrong thing.

    If you're running a national offer or targeting multiple markets, the starting point is higher. More geography means more competition for attention, and the platform needs more budget to find the right people across a larger pool. In those cases, we'll build a recommended budget around your specific footprint before anything goes live.

    Here's our rule regardless of scale: we will never ask you to increase your spend if the ads aren't working. The only time we push to scale is when something is clearly winning and we want to pour fuel on it.

    Your market size and location factor in too. We'll give you a specific recommendation based on your situation before we ever touch your budget.
